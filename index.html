The design

The room, as designed.

We built the General Session in our 3D design suite so you can see the room rather than picture it from a gear list. Four views from the suite, the front view again as a line drawing, and one photorealistic AI mockup of IMAG in use.

What the design is doing

Designed for the room, and for the camera.

What the renders show

Every seat has a clear view

The Formset backdrop carries the IVMF mark at center stage. A 16' × 9' screen sits either side, set up for rear projection so the projectors live behind the screens and out of every sightline. Two Sony broadcast cameras feed those screens, so a guest at a back table sees the speaker as clearly as one up front. Uplighting runs the length of both walls. All of it stands on ground-supported truss, so nothing hangs from the ceiling and there is no hotel rigging to pay for.

Who runs it

Our own technicians, on our own gear

The audio engineer, lighting designer, video technician and camera crew travel from Austin. They run this equipment every week because we own it. Stagehands for load-in and strike are hired locally in Dallas, which keeps travel cost down without putting unfamiliar people on the systems that matter.
